Start here

Z-City+ is a round-based gamemode. Every round picks a different mode, so one round you're hunting a murderer and the next you're holding off zombies or storming a building. Injuries are realistic, and most rounds give you one life.

Rounds

A new mode every round

When the round ends, the next mode is picked. Check the Game modes tab to see what each one asks of you. Players can vote on what comes next.

Your role

Read the role screen

At the start of each round a screen tells you your team, role and goal. Read it, because the goal changes from mode to mode.

Dead?

Spectate and wait

Dead players spectate until the next round. In some modes you can take over a bot or possess props while you wait.

Bots

Bots fill the server

Bots play every mode with you. Teammate bots follow orders from the radial menu and chat, like follow me, cover me, hold here or medic.

Menu

Press Esc for the Z-City menu

Settings, key binds, your character's appearance, achievements, stats and votes all live there.

Staying alive

There's no simple health bar to watch. Your body takes damage in parts, and what happens depends on where you were hit.

Bleeding

Stop the bleeding first

Wounds keep bleeding until you treat them. Bandages and other medical items are in your inventory or the shop. Lose too much blood and you pass out, then die.

Pain

Pain knocks you down

Too much pain and you collapse or pass out. Painkillers help you keep going.

Bones

Broken legs, broken aim

Broken legs slow you down and broken arms make aiming harder. Falls from high up can kill outright.

Ragdolls

Getting knocked over

Big hits and explosions can ragdoll you. While down you can grab things with your hands and get back up once you're able.

Help

Carry the wounded

Downed teammates can be dragged or carried to safety. Ask a bot to carry you from the radial menu if you're stuck.
